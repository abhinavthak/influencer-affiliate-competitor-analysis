Influencer Affiliate Programmes: Competitor Analysis

What the strongest creator programmes (Chewy, Amazon, Sephora, Gymshark, Walmart, Myntra, Nykaa) do, and how to turn that into an influencer programme for online degrees, starting with JGU Online. Read section 0 for the answer and section 7 for the plan.

0.Summary

At a glance

Question
What do the best influencer affiliate programmes do, and what should ours look like for online degrees?
Answer
The strongest programmes are retail, not education. They share six building blocks (2): a creator storefront, links plus codes, a live dashboard, an open door with an invite-only top tier, bonuses for consistent creators, and free product so creators can speak from experience.
The gap
No one runs this playbook for degrees. Coursera, the closest learning brand, pays affiliates up to ~45% on courses but excludes degrees (3.10). Education creators today get one-off sponsorships or a bare link.
Recommendation
A JGU Creator Programme with three levels, from open affiliate up to an invite-only Creator Squad. Retail's tools are adapted to a long decision: programme storefronts, a longer credit window, a lead-stage bonus, and free access to a module or masterclass in place of free product (7).
How to read
Section 1 explains why we look at retail. Section 2 is the playbook, 3 the programme profiles, 4 the comparison and 5 the commission benchmarks. Section 6 adapts the playbook to degrees, 7 is the plan and 8 lists open questions.

2.The playbook: six building blocks

Every strong programme in section 3 combines most of these six.

Block 1

Creator storefront

A page the creator curates with their own picks. Chewy, Amazon, Sephora, LTK.

Block 2

Links plus codes

A link for posts and a spoken code for videos and lives. Chewy, Gymshark.

Block 3

Live dashboard

Creators see their clicks, sales and earnings themselves. Chewy, Nykaa, LTK.

Block 4

Open door, invite-only top tier

Anyone can join the affiliate tier. An exclusive cohort sits above it. Gymshark, Sephora.

Block 5

Bonuses for consistency

Extra pay for regular posting and sales, plus boosts at peak times. Walmart, Amazon Prime Day.

Block 6

Free product

Creators use the product before they talk about it. Chewy's nano-creators, Gymshark athletes.

2.1The creator ladder

The best programmes move creators upward instead of treating all of them the same:

Open affiliateAnyone who meets a low bar joins, gets links and a dashboard. Nykaa: 5K+ followers. Myntra: no minimum. [Nykaa · Social Samosa]
Rewarded regularBonuses and higher rates for consistent posting and sales. Walmart tiered bonus, Myntra 15% for top performers. [Walmart · Otto]
Invite-only cohortA small group, paid and supported for a year. Sephora Squad, Gymshark athletes. [Tubefilter · BixGrow]

3.Programme profiles

One card per programme. Each fact links to its source. Each card ends with what to borrow for JGU.

3.1Chewy

Chewy · Chewpanions and Chewy StorefrontsPets, USOfficialTrade press
How it works
Creators curate a storefront of pet products, share custom links and earn commission when followers buy. They also get exclusive creator perks. [Chewy]
Storefronts
Launched in 2024 as an extension of the influencer programme. More than 600 creators by Nov 2025, still taking applications. Creators build multi-brand collections tailored to breed, size and type of pet. [Retail Dive · Net Influencer]
Tools
A dedicated performance dashboard and a unique sales code for followers, on top of affiliate links. [Retail Dive]
Commission
Flat $15 per new-customer order, which Chewy says equals about 8% for most partners. [Geniuslink]
Seeding
Creators receive real Chewy products tied to their own pet's needs (senior-dog joint supplements, kitten starter kits), not generic gift boxes. [Influencers Time]
Why it works
  • Storefronts let creators recommend for their own audience, by breed and size
  • A code works in video, where links don't
  • Paying more for new customers rewards real growth
Watch out
  • Built for repeat, low-cost orders
  • Commission details aren't on the public page
Borrow: a storefront for each creator, organised by career goal instead of breed ("MBA for working engineers", "Policy careers"), plus a code for every creator, and a reward that counts only new students.

3.2Amazon

Amazon Influencer ProgramMarketplace, globalThird-party
How it works
A personal shoppable storefront and shoppable videos, on top of normal affiliate links. [LoudCrowd]
Commission
Amazon's standard rates apply to traffic creators send: kitchen 4.5%, apparel 4%, home, beauty and toys 3%, luxury beauty 10%. [Creatorflow] Videos placed on Amazon itself pay 0–5% in the US. [Viralvue]
Boosts
Up to double the usual rates in 13 categories from 1 to 20 July 2025, around an extended Prime Day. [Tubefilter · Hello Partner]
Why it works
  • Creator videos appear where buyers are deciding
  • Time-limited boosts drive bursts of content
Watch out
  • Low rates; volume does the work
  • Rate tables change often
Borrow: put creator videos on JGU's own programme pages, where students compare options. Run a commission boost in the weeks before each intake deadline.

3.3Sephora

Sephora · #SephoraSquad and My Sephora StorefrontBeauty, USTrade press
Squad
A yearlong, paid programme that gives emerging creators resources and mentorship in exchange for campaign work. [Tubefilter] We found no public pay figures.
Selection
About 16,000 completed applications in 2019, backed by more than 250,000 testimonials from applicants' own followers on Instagram. [Glossy] 16,000+ applications for the 2023 cohort. [Archive] Cohorts are deliberately diverse: 79% of the 2021 class identified as people of colour. [Tubefilter]
Storefront
My Sephora Storefront, an affiliate storefront, launched in Sep 2025. [Retail Dive]
Why it works
  • Being chosen is itself a reward and makes good publicity
  • Follower testimonials show trust, not just reach
Watch out
  • Costs a lot per creator; only works for a small group
Borrow: a yearly JGU Creator Squad of 10–15 creators. They apply with testimonials from their followers, and JGU faculty mentor them.

3.4Gymshark

Gymshark · athletes and affiliatesFitness, UK and globalThird-party
Athletes
A separate, invitation-only programme. Gymshark's partnerships team scouts fitness creators whose values match the brand. [BixGrow · Referly]
Affiliates
Open to all through affiliate networks such as Rakuten Advertising and Awin, with a 30-day cookie. [BixGrow · Awin] Reported rates differ: 4% and 6%. [BixGrow · AffiliateRoll] Rates vary
Why it works
  • Two doors: a wide open one and a small exclusive one
  • Athletes become the face of the brand
Watch out
  • The open affiliate tier gets little support
Borrow: keep the open affiliate tier and the invite-only tier clearly separate. Choose the invite-only creators for fit with the programme, not follower count.

3.5Walmart

Walmart CreatorRetail, USOfficialRates third-party
How it works
Launched in 2022. [Retail Dive] Creators earn on eligible products bought through their links. A gamified, milestone-based bonus rewards consistent posting and strong sales. Creators also get storefronts and full sales insights. [Walmart]
Credit window
One click earns commission on everything the shopper buys for 14 days. Commission is locked for 30 days in case of returns and paid monthly through Impact. [Creatorflow]
Rates
Up to 18% via Walmart Creator. [Argil] Creatorflow notes that rate cards circulating online are unsourced; real rates are visible only after logging in. [Creatorflow] Unverified
Why it works
  • Pays for regular posting, not just sales
  • A wide credit window keeps creators happy
Watch out
  • Rates are not clearly published
Borrow: a monthly bonus for consistent posting, and credit for any JGU programme the student enrols in, not only the one the creator promoted. Hold commission until the refund window closes.

3.6LTK

LTK (formerly rewardStyle)Creator platformThird-party
How it works
A platform that sits between many brands and creators. Each brand sets its own rate. Creators apply directly, and LTK favours consistent, niche-focused creators over large follower counts. [InstantDM]
Rates
Usually 5–15% depending on the brand, up to 25%+ for premium retailers. [InstantDM]
Borrow: this is the long-term model for upGrad. Build one creator platform, then add each partner university as a "brand" with its own rates.

3.7Myntra

Myntra · Ultimate Glam Clan and affiliatesFashion, IndiaTrade press
Glam Clan
Launched Sep 2024. Shoppers post photo and video reviews, and earn commission when another user buys through them. No follower minimum. About 1M shopper-creators by Jun 2025 and ~3.5M by Nov 2025; 66% are Gen Z. [Social Samosa]
Affiliates
Creators who promote Myntra on Instagram, YouTube and Snapchat. [Social Samosa · Pulse] Reported rates are 10% base and 15% for top performers, with a 30-day cookie. [Otto] Unverified
Results
Social commerce is 10%+ of Myntra's revenue. Engaging with shopper-creator content raises conversion by 25%, according to Myntra's CMO. [Social Samosa]
Borrow: turn students and alumni into creators. Their reviews and "a week in my online MBA" videos convert because they're real. This is our alumni level (7.2).

3.8Nykaa

Nykaa · Affiliate Program (NAP)Beauty, IndiaOfficialTrade press
Terms
8% on every purchase, the same for all affiliates and categories. Open to creators with 5K+ followers, who generate links from a dashboard. [Nykaa]
Scale
100k+ influencers and affiliates, and 2M+ pieces of content (Feb 2026). Its Nykaaland event drew 3,000+ creators and 10,000+ pieces of content. [BuzzInContent]
Partnerships
YouTube Shopping Affiliate programme [Indian Television], and a beauty-creator incubator with Snapchat. [BuzzInContent]
Borrow: one simple published rate for the open level, plus events (a JGU campus or online day for creators) that produce a burst of content.

3.9MasterClass and Skillshare

MasterClass · SkillshareOnline learningThird-party
MasterClass
20% per sale with a 30-day cookie, through FlexOffers. [Lasso · FlexOffers]
Skillshare
Disputed: 40% in older listings [Monetizedeal], and 20% capped at $34 per new customer in a recent check of Skillshare's own page. [Ancaster] Unverified
Borrow: learning brands pay much higher percentages than retail (about 20–45% against 3–18%), because selling a learning product costs little to deliver once it's built.

3.10Coursera

Coursera Affiliate ProgramOnline learningThird-party
Commission
Up to 45% per sale, 30-day cookie. [Creator Hero] Tiered between about 15% and 45%, with bundles and Specializations near 20%. [Xark]
Degrees
Degrees are excluded from the affiliate programme. [Whop]
The gap: even the biggest online learning platform doesn't pay creators for degree enrolments. A creator programme built for degrees has no direct competitor today.

6.From retail to degrees

A degree differs from a basket of shopping in four ways, and each changes how we use the playbook.

DifferenceRetail approachOur version
Weeks to decide, not minutes14–30 day credit window (Walmart, Gymshark)A 60–90 day credit window, plus a small lead-stage bonus so creators see money before the enrolment closes.
Costs lakhs, not hundredsA percentage of the basketA flat ₹ amount per paid enrolment for each programme (5.1).
Nothing to post in the mailFree product (Chewy, Gymshark)Free access to a course module or faculty masterclass, a campus or online day, and time with the programme director.
A counsellor makes the saleBuyer checks out aloneThe creator's storefront leads to a counsellor who knows who referred the student. The creator's dashboard shows each lead's stage: lead, application, paid.

6.1Rules creators must follow

  • Disclosure: #ad or a paid-partnership label on every post. ASCI found 98% of influencer ads it reviewed needed changes, and education is its most non-compliant category. [Storyboard18 · Careers360]
  • Approved claims only: creators use JGU-approved wording ("Institution of Eminence" [JGU Online]) and never say "UGC approved" until JGU confirms it in writing.
  • No guarantees: no job, salary or "last few seats" promises.
  • Marketing only: JGU owns admissions and fees. UGC bans franchise arrangements for online degrees. [Nishith Desai]

7.Recommendation: JGU Creator Programme

7.1What we build

  • Programme storefronts (Chewy, LTK): each creator gets a page with their picks among JGU programmes, a personal video and a counsellor button.
  • Link plus spoken code (Chewy, Gymshark) for YouTube, lives and podcasts.
  • Creator dashboard (Nykaa, Chewy): clicks, leads, applications, enrolments and earnings, updated from the CRM.
  • Videos on JGU pages (Amazon): the best creator videos appear on the matching programme pages.
  • Intake boosts (Amazon Prime Day): higher commission in the last weeks before each intake deadline.

7.2Three levels

LevelWhoWhat they getModelled on
Open affiliateCareer, finance, tech, policy and teaching creators with 5K+ followersFlat ₹ per enrolment, small lead bonus, storefront, dashboardNykaa, Chewy

Alongside these, a student and alumni track (Myntra Glam Clan) has no follower minimum and runs on the existing Refer and Earn system.

7.390-day pilot

WeeksWork
1–3Agreement with JGU, approved claims, storefront template, codes, dashboard. Recruit 20 open affiliates and 5 Squad creators for MBA, BBA, AI & Data Science and Public Policy.
4–10Content goes live. One creator day with JGU faculty. Run an intake boost. Review the funnel weekly.
11–13Compare cost per enrolment with paid ads. Move the best open affiliates up to Rising. Pitch the results to the next partner university.

8.Open questions

  1. JGU commission. What does JGU pay per enrolment, by programme? This sets the flat creator payouts (5.2).
  2. Creators under JGU's brand. Does the agreement let us pay outside creators to promote under JGU's brand?
  3. Free access. Will JGU give creators a free module, masterclass or creator day?
  4. Accreditation wording. Which approvals can creators name, word for word?
  5. Lead status from the CRM. Can the CRM send each lead's stage back to power the creator dashboard?
  6. Commission codes and fee discounts. Should a creator's code also give the student a fee benefit? If so, JGU must approve it.
